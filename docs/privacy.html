Skate

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Skate is a game you play on your own device. This policy covers Skate on iPhone and iPad (from the App Store) and on Android (from Google Play), and explains what the app does with information. The short version: it does not collect any.

What the app collects

Nothing. Skate does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The app:

  • has no accounts and never asks who you are;
  • makes no network connections of its own;
  • contains no links to websites;
  • contains no advertising;
  • contains no analytics or tracking tools;
  • includes no third-party software that collects data.

What is stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information on your device only:

  • Your progress: your best score at each spot, the goals you've done and the letters you've found.
  • Your skater: which skater you picked and their colors and board.
  • Settings: whether sound and music are on.
  • A note of what you have bought, so anything you unlock opens right away before Apple or Google confirms your purchases.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and the developer cannot see it. If you back up your device with Apple's or Google's own backup services, it may be included in that backup under their terms.

Purchases

The app offers optional tips. On iPhone and iPad these are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account; on Android, entirely by Google Play through your Google account. The app never sees your name, payment details or address; it only learns from Apple or Google whether a purchase went through.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app from your device deletes everything it has stored.

Children

Skate does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what is described here, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
