Skate

An 8-bit skateboarding game you play with one thumb, set in Portland. By Tuesday Games.

Support

For help, a bug report or a suggestion, email info@tuesdaygames.com. It helps to mention your device, which spot you were skating and what happened.

How to play

The skater rolls on their own. The Tutorial on the title screen teaches every move, one at a time. In brief:

  • Tap to ollie. Hold for a higher one.
  • Swipe up or down on the ground to change lanes.
  • In the air: tap to grab, hold for a long grab, swipe left for a kickflip, right for a heelflip, down for a shove-it and up for a 360 flip. Land before the board comes back around and you bail.
  • Land on a rail, ledge, bench or curb to grind it. On a rail, swipe down to switch to a boardslide, tap to hop off, or swipe up to ollie out into the next lane.
  • Keep the tricks coming and they build a combo. Every trick raises the multiplier. Bail and the combo is lost.

Common questions

What are the goals?

Every spot has five, listed before each run and on the pause menu: a score, the letters hidden there, a named gap and two challenges. Once done, a goal stays done.

What is a gap?

A named move at each spot, like jumping a bench and the manhole after it in one go, or grinding one rail and landing on the next. The goals screen gives a short hint for each one, and pulling one off adds a big bonus to your combo.

Where are the letters?

The letters of PORTLAND are hidden across the spots, some floating over rails and some high enough to need a big ollie. Letters you find stay found. Spell the whole name for a special board.

How do I change my skater?

Tap Skater on the title screen. Pick Pat, Sammy or Angie the dog, and their colors and board.

There's no sound.

Skate follows your device's silent switch, like most games. Turn silent mode off and check the volume.

The phone does not vibrate.

Haptics follow your iPhone's settings: check Settings, then Sounds & Haptics, then System Haptics. iPads do not have haptics.

What does the tip jar do?

Tips are optional and unlock nothing. They help us keep making games. Thank you!
